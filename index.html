Parent guide

Speech & language milestones

Explore important milestones in speech and language development and learn effective parenting tips.

  • Development of Speech and Language

    Speech & language is an essential part of any child's development. Language development impacts your child's social interactions, behavior, and academic skills.

  • Speech milestones

    Birth – 12 months

    Birth – 6 months
    Cries, coos in response to voice and smiles.
    6 – 9 months
    Babbles, responds to name, shouts to attract attention.
    10 – 11 months
    Imitates sounds, says “mama/papa” without meaning.
    12 months
    Says 1–2 words, points to objects.

  • Speech milestones

    13 months – 2 years

    13 – 15 months
    Understands simple instructions, uses “mama”, “papa” and other words.
    16 – 18 months
    Uses 10–20 words, starts combining words.
    19 – 24 months
    Uses 50 words, two-word phrases.

  • Speech milestones

    2 – 4 years

    2 – 2.5 years
    Uses 400–450 words, two-three-word phrases.
    2.5 – 3 years
    Uses plurals, past tense, counts three objects.
    3 – 4 years
    Tells a story, knows colors, shapes, asks “why” and “who” questions.

  • For parents

    Tips for Parents

    • Talk to your child from birth.
    • Respond to baby's coos and babbling.
    • Play simple games with your baby.
    • Use gestures along with words.
    • Expand on what your child says.
    • Look at family photos and talk about them.
    • Ask lots of simple questions.
    • Describe your child's daily activities.

Who can use this app?

Parents or any family members can use this app, sit with their child and help the child improve their speaking ability step by step. If the child is not talking age appropriately due to reasons such as Autism, Deafness, ADHD, ADD, hearing impairment, behavioural issues, Down syndrome etc, this app will be helpful.

What are the prerequisites for the parent to use the app?

A parent with basic knowledge of mobile can use this app. The most important quality needed for the parent is patience. The process is a long journey, and parents need to be disciplined and patient.

Are you replacing traditional methods of speech therapy?

No, traditional methods are the best ways to give therapy. This will augment and complement the traditional method and improve the speech development faster.

My child is normal, should I still use this app?

Yes, our Accelerated Speech Program can improve the child's speaking ability further and thereby make the child a smart communicator.

Can I take the Online Speech therapy from Arcohz?

Yes, we provide Online Speech Therapy. We recommend that a combination of Online Speech Therapy and the activity module of the app will provide the best outcome for the child.

Don't you think using a mobile to teach a child is harmful?

Using a mobile for a small period of time for intensive interaction (both ways of communication) along with the involvement of parents will be helpful. It is recommended to use a tablet for the same.

My child is having a stuttering or voice disorder or misarticulation, can I use the app?

No, currently the app is only for Delayed Speech and Language.

Where is your offline center?

We only have 1 offline center in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
